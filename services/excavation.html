Careful digging around real homes

Excavation is not just moving dirt. Around a home, access is tighter, lawns and driveways matter, and the work often needs to be ready for another trade.

We plan the dig, keep spoil piles sensible, protect the surrounding area where possible, and leave the site ready for the next step.

  • Small foundation and exterior access digs
  • Residential trenching and service preparation
  • Careful machine work near homes, fences, and landscaping
  • Tidy backfill and cleanup planning
